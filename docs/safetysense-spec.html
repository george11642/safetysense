SafetySense.

Help that comes to you when you can't reach your phone.

During a Guard session, SafetySense listens and watches on your phone only. If you go quiet, can't answer, or lose your phone, it brings in your people and then professional dispatch, with no tap from you.

The problem

Most safety apps assume you can press a button.

People freeze

In a study of 298 women seen after a sexual assault, 70% reported significant tonic immobility, an involuntary inability to move (Möller et al., 2017). A panic button doesn't help someone who has frozen.

Drugs remove the choice

Someone who's been drugged may not recognize danger or be able to unlock a phone.

Phones get taken

A timer-based app may not react until the timer runs out. If SafetySense's heartbeat stops mid-session, its server raises the alert itself, designed to reach contacts in about 3 to 4 minutes.

Even texting 911 takes a phone in your hand

Calling or texting 911 both need you to act. SafetySense's data-only path to dispatch starts by itself. (Corrected Nov 13, 2026: an earlier version said New Mexico has no text-to-911. It does.)

How it works

Turn it on. It watches. It decides. Help comes.

1 · ARM

Start a Guard session

Tap, use the Action Button, or accept SafetySense's suggestion when you arrive at a bar on a Friday night.

2 · WATCH

On-device sensing

SafetySense checks sound, words, motion, and location. Audio stays in a 30-second memory buffer that's never written to storage.

3 · DECIDE

Risk engine

Rules you can explain make the decision, and the on-device AI gives a second opinion. SafetySense checks in with you before raising the alert.

4 · RESPOND

People, then dispatch

Your contacts get a live-location link. If nobody can confirm you're safe, professional monitoring sends police.

Detection

Many weak signals add up to one clear decision.

Every signal is processed on the phone. Only the server's heartbeat check runs off the phone, because it has to keep working after the phone is gone.

Risk engine

Weighted rules that fade over time

Weights depend on mode and time of day. Settings are Relaxed, Balanced, or Vigilant.

On-device LLM second opinion on iPhone 15 Pro and later. It can nudge the score, but it can never trigger an alert alone or block an SOS.

Escalation ladder

SafetySense checks in with you before it brings in anyone else.

Step through a sample Night Out to see what SafetySense does at each level.

0Guarding

Listening, nothing saved

A 30-second rolling buffer in memory. The Live Activity shows the session is running.

stop: end session (Face ID)
1Check-in

"You okay?"

A quiet haptic and a 45-second ring. Triggered by the risk score or a scheduled check-in.

stop: tap "I'm okay"
2Alert

Your people are notified

A text and automated call with a live map. Evidence is encrypted and uploaded while it records. Also triggered by the safe word, the Action Button, or a lost heartbeat.

stop: safety PIN
3Dispatch

Professional help is sent

After 90 seconds with no valid cancel, or when a contact taps "Send help." The monitoring center calls a SafetySense relay number that forwards to you, then sends police to your live location.

stop: PIN plus the dispatcher's call

Duress PIN. If someone forces you to cancel, enter your duress PIN. SafetySense shows "Cancelled" on screen and goes straight to Dispatch. Dispatch is given a relay number, not your phone, and once the server has the duress PIN, the relay never connects a call to your phone. This is tested with simulated calls and the Noonlight sandbox, not a real dispatcher call. Production needs a pool of relay numbers and Noonlight's confirmation that a relay is allowed.

Key screens

Calm by default, impossible to miss in an emergency.

Modes

Each situation gets its own level of protection.

Everyday

Always on, no mic

SOS triggers, trusted contacts, medical info, and campus resources.

Walk Home

Live escort

One contact follows you live. SafetySense alerts them if you leave your route, stop too long, or don't arrive.

Night Out

Full Guard

On-device audio AI, motion, check-ins every 30–90 minutes, and drink-check prompts.

Date

Sealed details

Their name, photo, and the venue are encrypted. Your friend sees them only if an alert fires.

Ride

Trip watch

Log the plate and driver. SafetySense alerts you if the route changes or the car makes an unplanned stop.

What changed from the first idea

Same goal, adjusted to what iOS allows and what users will trust.

AI listens all the time
Guard sessions you start yourself. SafetySense suggests a session when the context fits. iOS can't start the mic from the background, the battery cost is real, and App Review rejects always-on recording.
Press the side button
Action Button, Lock Screen control, Siri, safe word. Apple doesn't let apps use the side button. These are the official ways to trigger an app.
AI calls the police
The AI raises the alert level; it doesn't dial 911 alone. iOS can't place calls without a tap, so dispatch goes through professional monitoring, which verifies before sending police.
Detect being drugged
Detect being unable to respond. SafetySense watches for missed check-ins, unsteady movement, and falls. No phone can detect a drug, so SafetySense doesn't promise it.
Copy every feature of Lobo Guardian
Cover the basics well and put the effort into proactive protection. UNM Safe replaced LoboGuardian (retired Jul 31, 2026), and SafetySense links to it.
Privacy & security

Private by design.

On the phone

  • Audio, transcripts, and AI reasoning never leave the device while guarding
  • The 30-second buffer stays in memory and is overwritten continuously
  • Face ID locks the Vault. Optional discreet app icon
  • Duress PIN

On the server

  • Evidence is encrypted (AES-GCM) before upload, so the server stores only data it can't read
  • Each clip has a SHA-256 hash and timestamp to show it hasn't been altered
  • Location is kept only during sessions and alerts, and auto-deleted after 30 days
  • No ad or analytics SDKs. App Attest on dispatch is planned
