SafetySense

Calls for help when you can't. During a night out, a walk home, or a first date, SafetySense watches for signs of danger on your phone, then brings in your people, then professional dispatch.

1 · CHECK-IN

Your phone asks if you're okay. A quick tap or your PIN clears it.

2 · ALERT

Your trusted contacts get a call and a live-location link with "I'm on my way" and "Send help" buttons.

3 · DISPATCH

If no one cancels, professional monitoring is asked to send help to your live location.

If your phone goes dark

During a session your phone checks in with SafetySense every minute. If those check-ins stop without a clean "End session", the server alerts your contacts with your last known location.

SafetySense supplements 911; it never replaces it. If you or someone else is in danger, call 911. Confidential support in Albuquerque is also available through UNM's LoboRESPECT Advocacy Center and the Rape Crisis Center of Central New Mexico.

Got a SafetySense link in a text? Open the full link from that message to see their live status.

Built by George and Mary Teifel. Privacy first: audio never leaves the phone unless an alert fires, and live links expire when the alert ends.
