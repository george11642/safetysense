## What's in the file

- **Cover**: title frame. It's set as the file thumbnail if Figma kept the change; check the file browser.
- **Flow overview**: every screen as a thumbnail, with tap arrows and auto-advance arrows across eight lanes (plus 20b and 20c beside 20, and 20eb and 20ec beside 20e): onboarding, core, date + contact view, Walk Home + Ride, tabs + alert timeline, the three promise cards, the per-mode check-ins, and a PIN typed on the countdown (13e, 19e, 20e).
- **Prototype**: 56 iPhone screens (402×874) plus 8 overlays. Device: iPhone 17 Pro. Every visible button, chip and tab does something; nothing dead-ends.
  - Flows / starting points: `1 · Full demo (start here)`, `2 · Night Out → Alert (skip onboarding)`, `3 · Date mode`, `4 · Contact live link`, `5 · Walk Home and Ride modes`.
  - Start screen: "Start the prototype" runs the full demo. The flow chips (Onboarding, Night Out, Check-in, Alert, Duress PIN, Date mode, Contact live link, Support, Walk Home, Ride) jump straight to that part, and each one resets the demo state for its own mode (Walk Home, Ride and Date set that mode, so later Home tabs and alerts follow it).
  - Onboarding: Start → Welcome → Three promises (three cards: Next → Next → Sounds good) → Trusted contacts → Permissions → Two PINs → Practice drill → (Run the drill shows the TEST result) → Home. The steps are labelled 2 to 5 of 5 and the five-segment progress bar matches (it fills across the three promise cards). On Permissions, each Allow flips that row to On; Allow all turns both on and continues, and once both rows are On the main button reads Continue (Not now disappears). The Safety PIN box starts empty; tapping either box opens a PIN pad, and any key fills that box's four dots ("PIN set") and closes the pad. Settings later shows "Safety & duress PINs: On" only when both boxes were filled; otherwise it says Not set, Safety only or Duress only (Skip on Two PINs or on Permissions leaves it Not set). The back chevron goes back. Skip on the three promise cards goes to Trusted contacts (choosing who to call is the one required step); Skip on later steps goes to Home.
  - Home: the Walk Home, Night Out, Date and Ride chips all switch modes instantly (the selected chip, heading and Start Guard label change) and remember the mode. Edit or the contacts card opens Trusted contacts. The tab bar opens Contacts, Vault, Support and Settings, and their Home tab returns to the Home screen of the mode you picked. Every tap on a Home screen records that screen's mode, so this also holds when the viewer starts on Date or Walk Home (flows 3 and 5) without tapping a chip. Support and Settings scroll to the end of the real app's list (stitched simulator screenshots) under a fixed tab bar; every hotline button, Open my Vault and "In danger right now? Call 911" do something. The app's internal Developer row is hidden on Settings, and the Settings list ends cleanly on one "Version 0.1.0 (1)" line above the tab bar. Every row on Settings, including the ones below the first screen, opens the prototype note.
  - Core (Night Out): Home → Start Guard → Guard active (0:06 elapsed, next check-in 2:10 PM) → (auto-advances after 4 s) Check-in (the seconds count down from 20) → "I'm okay" goes to Guard · checked in (20:12 elapsed, next check-in 2:30 PM). "I need help", or no answer for 20 s, goes to Alert countdown (counts down from 15, and restarts at 15 on every visit, including from the Start screen's Alert chip; Maya gets a call and a text; Jordan and Sam are texted) → (Send help now, or 15 s) Alert active → I'm safe now → Enter PIN → Safety PIN → Marked safe → Find support → Support → Home. "Back to home" on both Marked safe screens returns to the Home screen of the mode the alert came from (Walk Home, Night Out, Date or Ride). Both Guard screens have a visible End Guard button. Alert active scrolls: below the map are all three contacts (Maya answered the call, Jordan and Sam got texts) and the alert timeline; the dock has I'm safe now and Call 911 (opens the no-call note). On the countdown and on Enter PIN only the number keys count as a PIN; backspace does nothing (there is nothing to delete). "Not now" on Enter PIN, opened from the countdown, restarts the countdown at 15; opened from Alert active it goes back to the alert. Enter PIN opened from the countdown says "Enter your safety PIN to stop the alert. Help hasn't been requested yet."; opened from Alert active it says the monitoring center may still call. A PIN typed on the countdown, before dispatch, leads to 13e ("ALERT 2:11 · STOPPED PRE-DISPATCH") or, for the duress PIN, 19e and then Maya's page 20e with a 2:11 PM cancel; a PIN from Alert active keeps the 2:11–2:31 summary on 13/19. On 20e the timeline is newest first (2:12 dispatch requested above the 2:11 duress cancel) and the page header says Updated 2:12:10 PM; battery reads 62% on every 2:11–2:13 live link (20e, 20eb, 20ec, 20ed, 20ed2 and 18). On 18 the header follows Maya's responses: Updated 2:13:10 PM, then 2:13:20 PM after her first response and 2:13:40 PM after her second (matching the 2:13:20 and 2:13:40 rows on Alex's phone). The Risk card, the status chips on every Guard (Date guard included), the whole arrival card and Vault clip rows open the prototype note; the whole Ride details card opens the Ride sealed-details note (O2r: Uber, Silver Camry, BXR 482, driver Daniel).
  - Alert context: the alert remembers which Guard you were in and why it started (no answer, or "I need help"), using prototype variables. The countdown's "Why" line, the mode chip on Alert active, the summary on Marked safe ("RIDE · ALERT 2:11–2:31 · 3 CLIPS IN VAULT") and the contact's live link (reason line plus that mode's sealed details: route and ETA, plate and driver, or the date's name) all follow it. Night Out with "no answer" is the default.
  - Walk Home: Home → Walk Home → Start Guard → Walk setup (destination + 30 min, arrival check 2:20 PM; Maya follows along, and all three contacts are alerted if you don't arrive) → Start walking → Guard · Walk Home (started 1:50 PM) → "I made it · End Guard" goes Home. The pink pill ("Simulate a long stop") or the ring opens Walk Home's own check-in; "I'm okay" returns to the walk with a quiet confirmation and the arrival check still counting down, and "I need help" (or no answer) starts a Walk Home alert.
  - Ride: Home → Ride → Start Guard → Ride setup (service, plate, driver, car; arrival check 2:25 PM) → Start Ride guard → Guard · Ride → "I've arrived · End Guard" goes Home. The pill ("Simulate an unexpected stop") or the ring opens Ride's own check-in; "I'm okay" returns to the ride, and an alert from here shows the plate and driver on the live link.
  - Date mode: Home → Date → Start Guard → Date setup (name and optional photo, sealed) → Start Date guard → Date guard. The Date guard checks in every 20 minutes like Night Out (0:06 elapsed, next check-in 2:10 PM in 19 min, 54 sec); after "I'm okay" it shows 20:12 elapsed, next check-in 2:30 PM. The sealed card ("Chris"; the photo stays on the phone) previews what contacts would see only if an alert fires; the ring or the pink "Prototype · Simulate a check-in" pill jumps to Date mode's own check-in, and "I'm okay" returns to the Date guard (never to Night Out); End Date guard goes Home. Fields on the three setup sheets open the prototype note. Every check-in starts at 20 and the dispatch countdown at 15 on every visit (the prototype resets component state when it opens those screens).
  - Duress branch: Enter PIN → Duress PIN → Marked safe (looks identical apart from a pink prototype pill) → (6 s, or tap the pill) the contact's live link: "Still active. The cancel on Alex's phone at 2:31 PM used their duress PIN, so help is still coming", with a 2:31 PM timeline entry → Restart demo. "I'm on my way" / "I called 911" on 20 switch to 20b / 20c with her response at 2:31 PM; on 20b and 20c the confirmed one is a status label, and tapping the other one adds it without removing the first (20d: on the way, then called 911; 20d2: the reverse). Both rows stay in What's happened (newest of Maya's two on top, each with its own time), both buttons become status labels, and nothing can be un-done (Maya can never "un-call" 911). On 20e they switch to 20eb / 20ec (her response at 2:12 PM, header Updated 2:12:40 PM), and the second button leads to 20ed / 20ed2 with both 2:12 PM rows. On every duress page the first timeline entry, Alert started, names why the alert started (for example "Missed check-in during Night Out" or "Tapped “I need help” during Walk Home"). If Maya had already tapped "I'm on my way" or "I called 911" on her live link, this page is 20b or 20c instead: the same page with her 2:13 PM response kept in the timeline (20d / 20d2 if she tapped both).
  - Contact live link (web): Alert active → Share link (or the Your people card) → scrollable live-link page (Maya's view, with an iOS status bar). Under the reason line the dispatch note follows straight on. Maya hasn't responded yet; "I'm on my way" / "I called 911" switch back to Alex's alert (11b, 11c) showing that response in the people list and timeline. Responses add up rather than replace each other: if she then opens her page again and taps the other button, Alex's alert shows both (11d: on the way 2:13:20, called 911 2:13:40; 11d2: the reverse), and her page lists both rows and shows both buttons as confirmed. "Back to Alex's phone" returns to Alert active as it was: 11 if Maya hasn't answered, 11b or 11c after one response, 11d or 11d2 after both. Maya's page reads 2:13, so coming back to 11 moves its clock, header and evidence line from 2:12 to 2:13 as well, and the round trip never runs backwards (a new alert from the countdown starts at 2:12 again). After Maya responds, her button on 18 shows "✓ You're on your way · Alex can see it" (or "✓ You called 911"), and 20b/20c show the same confirmed state. A confirmed button on 18 is only a status label: tapping it does nothing, so it can't add a response Maya never gave or swap the order of her two responses. Her own 2:13 PM response is also added to the top of 18's "What's happened" list. Dispatch is already requested, so the page says so instead of offering Send help now. Open in Maps, the map zoom buttons and the call-911 link open explanatory overlays. On all live-link pages the prototype pill is the same 44 px pill in a tall solid bottom bar, so it never covers page content, and the page scrolls far enough for the footer to clear it.
  - Contacts tab: tapping a contact row (or its ⋯ menu) opens the prototype note. "What your contacts see" opens an example live link marked "Preview · example" (nothing is happening); it shows no clock times ("Updated just now", events "1m ago" / "2m ago"), so it never disagrees with the phone clock; its buttons explain that nothing is sent, the sealed-details box says it is filled in for Walk Home, Date and Ride, and the pill goes back to Contacts.
  - Overlays: the Date guard's sealed card opens O2 ("Chris"), the Ride guard's opens O2r (ride, plate, driver); hotline and 911 buttons open "This prototype never places calls" (no tel: links anywhere); the PIN boxes open a PIN pad; buttons on the contact preview say nothing is sent; other app-only controls open a short prototype note (worded for both the app and the contacts' web page). Close, or tap outside the card.
- **Components**: two ticking countdown component sets (check-in 20→0, dispatch 15→0, one second per variant), a Button component set (Pink / Teal / Coral / Ghost, with Label text, Icon swap and Show icon properties) and 11 icon components.

Screen fills are the real app screenshots from `design/screens/`. The live-link pages were rendered from `web/a.html` with
fictional demo data on a consistent timeline (alert 2:11 PM, contacts called and texted 2:11 PM, dispatch 2:12 PM, Maya responds 2:13 PM, cancelled 2:31 PM; the Vault's three clips are 2:11:21, 2:11:51 and 2:12:21 PM; after a PIN typed on the countdown the Vault shows only the 2:11:21 PM clip, "1 clip · 0:30 total", because recording stops with the cancel; in that 1-clip layout only Clip 1, its ⋯ menu, Share with an advocate and Delete all evidence are tappable). The iOS status-bar clock on every screen follows the same story (onboarding 1:45–1:48; all four Home screens, setups and Guards start at 1:50; check-ins 2:10 (Night Out, Date) and 2:11 (Walk Home after a long stop, Ride after an unexpected stop); alert 2:11–2:13 (11 shows 2:12 on arrival and 2:13 after a visit to Maya's live link; 11b and 11c, after Maya responds, show 2:13 in the header and evidence line too); marked safe 2:31). The four Home screens use the same tabClock variable as the tabs, so time never runs backwards on the way home: Back to home shows 2:31 (2:11 after an early cancel), End Guard shows that Guard's clock (1:50, 2:10 or 2:11), and the Home tab after Find support keeps 2:32 / 2:12. Starting a new Guard after an alert is the one exception: setups and Guards are fixed at the 1:50 story, and ending that Guard (End Guard / End Date guard / I made it / I've arrived) also resets the Vault to the earlier Nov 4 alert with all three clips, so the Vault never lists an alert later than the phone clock. Elapsed, next check-in, arrival and time-left values agree with those clocks to the second. Screens shared across the story use prototype variables for their clock: the tabs (Contacts, Vault, Support, Settings) and the contacts preview show 1:50 when opened from Home before any alert and 2:32 (or 2:12 after an early cancel) when opened from Marked safe → Find support, and the Vault header then reads "Alert · today at 2:11 PM" instead of a past alert on Nov 4; Enter PIN shows 2:11 when opened from the countdown and, opened from Alert active, the same clock that alert showed (2:12, or 2:13 after a visit to Maya's live link), so "Not now" back to the alert never runs backwards; the clock moves on to 2:31 only when a PIN is entered (Marked safe).
