**Summary:**
SafetySense is an iPhone safety app for UNM and CNM students that gets help to them when they can't reach their phone. During a Guard session (a night out, a walk home, a first date or a ride), it listens for signs of danger such as screaming, a spoken safe word, a fall followed by stillness, or missed check-ins. All of this processing happens on the phone, and nothing is recorded unless something is wrong. If the student doesn't respond, SafetySense calls their trusted contacts with a live-location link and then hands off to professional dispatch. If the phone is smashed or switched off mid-session, the server raises the alert by itself. A duress PIN looks like cancelling, but the alert stays live. SafetySense supplements 911; it never replaces it. Our high-fidelity prototype is backed by a working iOS 26 build and a live backend.
