Who's building SafetySense

A two-person, brother-and-sister team across UNM and CNM. We're building a personal-safety app that gets help to a student when they can't reach their phone.

01What we've built so far

iOS app
Working SwiftUI prototype for iOS 26: onboarding, Guard sessions, check-ins, alert ladder, Vault and Support.
Live backend
Supabase edge API, Postgres, Vault and a heartbeat watchdog. Twilio voice calls to verified contacts work today.
Live link
A page for trusted contacts, no app needed: live location, status, and "I'm on my way" or "Send help".
How we split the work

George owns the engineering. Mary owns what users see and hear: research, wording, and flows. We review every screen together, with one test: would this feel safe and respectful to someone on the worst night of their life? SafetySense supplements 911. It never replaces it.
