App Contest Calendar for 2026

LOCATION

EVENT

DETAILS

Monday, August 17th from 10 am - 2 pm

In-Person at UNM Duck Pond

Kick Off-Welcome Back Days

Come by the UNM Information Technologies table and find out more details about the contest and register your interest.  Register your interest.

Thursday, September 17th from 3 pm - 6 pm

In-Person at UNM Rainforest

Kick Off / Meet the Mentors

Looking for team members and a mentor?  We will have technical mentors on site. Register for the Kick Off

Thursday, October 8th 3pm - 4:30pm

In-Person at UNM Rainforest

Mentoring Session

Meet with your mentors, get technical assistance, work with your team, and get questions answered, if you wish.  It is up to you/your team to contact your mentor. Register for the Mentor Meet Up.

Tuesday, October 20th 3pm - 4:30pm

IT Building 153, Conference Room 2001 or Online

Hybrid Mentoring Session

Meet with your mentors, get technical assistance, work with your team, and get questions answered, if you wish.  It is up to you/your team to contact your mentor. Register for the Mentor Meet Up.

Friday, October 30th 11:59 pm

Via online link

Registration Closes

Make sure your register by this deadline to participate in the contest. No materials are due yet. Just register so we can communicate with you.

Friday, November 6th 11:59 pm

Email your required submission materials

See Submission Criteria and Process

It is required that you email your documents in PDF or Word Document form to itevents@unm.edu.  The only link you include is a link to your prototype.  Otherwise, your submission should be in document form.

Monday, November 9th

Email

Notification of the Finalists

The program team will notify everyone of the finalist selection.

Mandatory Practice Session November 10th-13th

In-Person at UNM or CNM Campus – exact location TBD

Mandatory Practice Sessions

Finalists will be required to attend one mandatory practice session with the Program Committee prior to Presentation Day. This practice session will be scheduled when we contact you to schedule your Presentation Day time slot. YOU WILL BE REQUIRED TO BRING YOUR UNOFFICIAL TRANSCRIPT to this practice session.

Monday, November 16th at Noon (No Exceptions)

Email your final presentation materials

No changes accepted after this deadline!  See Submission Criteria and Process

Include all required materials. 

Friday, November 20th from 1 pm - 5 pm

In-Person at UNM Rainforest

Presentation Day/Awards Ceremony

Up to 10 finalists will be invited to pitch their app in front of a panel of judges.  The Awards Ceremony will take place after the judging has concluded.

Any information requests can be made to  itevents@unm.edu.
