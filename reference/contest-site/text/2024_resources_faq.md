# FAQ

Here you will find frequently asked questions that may help you during this contest. Check back regularly, as content will be updated throughout the contest.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

**Can I change my App’s name, team members, contact information, etc. ?**

Yes, we understand things change, just be sure to send updates to <appcontest@unm.edu> so that we have the most current information.  DEADLINE FOR UPDATES/CHANGES is November 6<sup>th</sup>, 2024.

**Do I need to bring my presentation on a USB? **

Yes, it is imperative that you bring your presentation on a USB!!!

**Can I bring my own device to Presentation Day? **

No.  Outside computers/devices are not allowed to connect to the UNM Rainforest system!!!

Any questions can be sent at any time to <appcontest@unm.edu>
