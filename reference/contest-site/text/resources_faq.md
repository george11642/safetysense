# FAQ

Here you will find frequently asked questions that may help you during this contest. Check back regularly, as content will be updated throughout the contest.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

**Can I change my App’s name, team members, contact information, etc. ?**

Yes, we understand things change, just be sure to send updates to <itevents@unm.edu> so that we have the most current information *until the first email submission deadline* see calendar for other important dates.

 

**Can I bring my own device to Presentation Day? **

No.  Outside computers/devices are not allowed to connect to the UNM Rainforest system!!!

 

**Can I invite my friends and family to Demo Day for my presentation? **

Yes.  Friends and family are welcome to attend your presentation.

 

**How do I turn in my unoffical transcript? **

Bring a hard copy to your mandatory mentoring session.

 

**Can I resubmit an app that I've presented in a previous year's App Contest?**

No.

 

**Can I submit a coded app instead of a prototype?**

No. You may start with a coded app, but in order to participate in the contest you will need to submit a prototype and pitch deck for your app.

\

Any questions can be sent at any time to <itevents@unm.edu>
