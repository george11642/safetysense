## Mission

This year's contest focuses on developing a plan and a pitch. Develop an idea and form a team to build a "high fidelity" prototype and design. Your submission will be your proposal and road map for further coding and development.

## General Contest Overview

1.  **Register **to participate and receive updates.
    - Must register no later than October 30, 2026 at 11:59 PM to be eligible to submit an entry in this year’s contest.
    - Kick Off and Meet the Mentors Session September 17, 2026 at UNM Rainforest Innovations
2.  **Develop** your idea, form a team.
3.  **Build a prototype**, review, plan, repeat.
4.  **Check In**, email questions, talk to your mentor, sign up for virtual prototyping session.
5.  **Submit** Prototype (or similar) plan, diagrams, documentation (see Submission Process).
6.  **Meet** with program team to refine your pitch, and review your materials (including a draft presentation) at your mandatory practice session.
7.  **Finalize** documents for Presentation Day.
8.  **Attend** the Presentation Day and Awards Ceremony.

Go to the [Calendar](calendar.html "UNM/CNM App Contest Calendar") for specific dates, [Rules](rules.html "UNM/CNM App Contest Rules") for eligibility and requirements, and [Resources](resources/helpful.html "UNM/CNM App Contest Resources") for additional support information.

 

<span id="removed-diff-8" class="diff-html-removed">UNM and CNM faculty and staff, along with experienced mentors will provide support to these projects through workshops and mentorship. UNM, CNM, and</span>  <span id="removed-diff-9" class="diff-html-removed">the City of Albuquerque have invested in open data sets which are available to make the apps more useful. Data sets include: UNM events, class schedule data, campus locations, Route 66 images, bike paths, list of local businesses, and more. </span>

<span id="removed-diff-10" class="diff-html-removed">Through this effort, we hope to inspire students to develop skills in concept, planning, design, development, collaboration, and presentation skills which are increasingly valuable in our information economy, and to yield apps that can improve an experience for our students and beyond.</span>
